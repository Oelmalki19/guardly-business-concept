# Guardly – Business Concept

Guardly is a university business concept for a digital platform that
connects customers with verified security guards for short-term personal
protection.

## Project Context

This project was created for the BWR module
(Business Administration and Law) at TH Köln during the summer semester 2026.

## Problem

Traditional security services can be expensive, inflexible, and difficult
to book for short periods. Guardly aims to provide a simpler digital booking
process for short-term security and personal accompaniment.

## Proposed Solution

The concept includes:

- Digital booking through an app
- Verified security guards
- Short-term bookings
- Payment integration
- Customer ratings
- Communication between customers and guards

## My Contribution

I was responsible for the operational and organizational concept of Guardly.

My sections included:

- Production and MVP concept
- Make-or-buy decision and degree of vertical integration
- Location choice and relevant location factors
- Organizational structure
- Legal-form selection and tax considerations
- Presentation of these sections as part of the final team presentation

## Key Decisions in My Sections

- Proposed a digital, service-based operating model with a focus on the
  core booking and matching functions.
- Defined the MVP features, including booking, payment integration, chat,
  and ratings.
- Recommended a Cologne headquarters with a remote-first operating model.
- Proposed a lean organizational structure with flat hierarchies and
  interdisciplinary collaboration.
- Recommended a UG (haftungsbeschränkt) as the initial legal form for
  limited liability with relatively low initial capital requirements.
